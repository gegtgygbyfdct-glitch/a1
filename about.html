Haute Couture Textile Craft

The Heritage & Science of Fine Fashion Threads

Stationed in Soho, Manhattan, ChicThreadBay was established to champion mulberry silk tailoring, evaluate organic cotton weaves, and provide an empirical guide for fashion connoisseurs.

Atelier Mandate

Authentic Textile Integrity Over Fast-Fashion Synthetic Blends

In a fashion industry inundated with cheap polyester synthetics that pill and pollute waterways, ChicThreadBay advocates for Grade 6A mulberry silk, hand-loomed European flax linen, and botanical indigo dyeing.

Our team of haute couture pattern makers, textile chemists, and fashion directors evaluate garments through rigorous laboratory trials: testing seam tensile strength, colorfastness under UV exposure, and bias-cut drape physics.

"True chic elegance begins at the fiber level—where natural luster, architectural seam strength, and hand-embroidered details create timeless heirlooms."

Atelier Principles

Our Four Haute Couture Standards

1. Mulberry Silk

22-momme weight Grade 6A long-staple silk delivering liquid sheen and fluid drape.

2. Botanical Dyes

100% natural plant-extracted pigments free of toxic heavy metals and synthetic fixatives.

3. Goldwork Stitching

Intricate metallic embroidery, Zardozi craftsmanship, and hand-bound buttonholes.

4. Objective Research

100% independent fashion evaluations backed by textile stress testing and drape analysis.
