Cookie Transparency

Cookie Usage & Browser Preferences

Effective August 2026.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when visiting a web application.
They enable websites to remember your navigation preferences and session settings.
We use cookies to ensure optimal performance across our digital fashion journal.
Cookies cannot run programs or deliver dangerous viruses to your personal computer.
They simply provide non-identifying data regarding how users interact with our site.
Understanding cookie deployment helps you make informed choices about digital privacy.

2. Types of Cookies We Deploys

Essential session cookies are required for basic site navigation and core accessibility.
Performance cookies anonymously track readership metrics and page loading speeds.
Functionality cookies remember your chosen layout settings and newsletter preferences.
Analytics cookies gather aggregated telemetry on article popularity and traffic sources.
All deployed cookies are regularly audited to ensure strict security and compliance.
We do not deploy invasive cross-site advertising cookies or personal profiling tools.

3. Google Analytics Integration

We utilize Google Tag (gtag.js) with container configuration property ID G-0LY0HY7L01.
This service uses first-party cookies to measure audience engagement and reader retention.
Google Analytics data is anonymized before server processing and statistical reporting.
No personal identifiers or sensitive form entries are transmitted to Google servers.
You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites via browser add-ons.
Review Google's privacy policy for comprehensive information on their data practices.

4. Managing Browser Cookie Settings

Most web browsers automatically accept cookies by default upon initial installation.
You can modify your browser settings to decline cookies or alert you when sent.
Disabling essential cookies may impair the display or functionality of certain pages.
Refer to your browser's help menu for detailed instructions on managing cookies.
Mobile devices also provide system settings to restrict application tracking identifiers.
Clearing your browser cache removes all currently stored cookie files from your device.
